Chunking is a semantic decision, not a technical one.

── PARAGRAPH-BASED
"…up to $500 provided the expense was

Overlap is for the LLM. Not for retrieval.

It barely changes similarity scores. What it does is stop chunk 7 from starting cold.

Without overlap

Chunk 7 starts cold

The LLM misses the condition set in paragraph 6. The answer is incomplete — or invented.

With 2-sentence overlap

Chunk 7 has context

The last 2 lines of paragraph 6 lead into chunk 7. The answer stays grounded.

[YES] PrasadLesson 5

ChromaDB isn't magic. It's two systems working as a team.

./chroma_store — on disk
./chroma_store/
├── chroma.sqlite3      ← ids, text, metadata
└── <collection-uuid>/
    └── data_level0.bin ← the vectors (HNSW)

HNSW does the math — finds the nearest vectors.
SQLite keeps the meaning — returns their text.
